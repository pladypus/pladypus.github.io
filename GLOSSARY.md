# Glossary

**Hub**: the site at pladypus.dev. Its main job is pointing visitors to the Tools; it also carries a short intro about the owner.

**Tool**: a public web app the owner built and deployed on its own `*.pladypus.dev` subdomain, listed on the Hub (e.g. Grouper, Mortgage). A project with a subdomain isn't a Tool until it's listed: the groomsmen site has a subdomain but isn't listed.
